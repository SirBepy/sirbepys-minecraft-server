Voice chat (optional)

Hear players near you, quieter the further away they are. It needs the Simple Voice Chat mod, which the modpack sets up for you along with more FPS and the food textures.

  1. Install the free Modrinth App or Prism Launcher.
  2. Download the modpack.
  3. Modrinth App: + → From file → pick the .mrpack. Prism: Add Instance → Import.
  4. Sign in, launch it, then press V in game to set up your mic.
New foods look wrong?

The server adds 200+ foods, but can't send you their textures. The modpack includes them. On plain Minecraft:

  1. Download "Reg's More Foods Pack" from its Modrinth page and unzip it.
  2. Keep the "Resourcepack" zip inside, still zipped.
  3. Options → Resource Packs → Open Pack Folder, drop it in, then move it to Selected.
Can't connect?
"Unknown host" or it won't connect
Try the backup address, port included: eu-de-p59-a6-cg.kineticpanel.net:25566
"Outdated client" or "Incompatible version"
Your game isn't on 26.3: see step 1.
Voice chat says disconnected
Press V → Settings and check your mic. School or work Wi-Fi and some VPNs block it.
Still nothing
The server may be restarting. Ask Joe.
