What's different

Survival like you know it, plus a few friendly extras.

Settle

  • Towns

    Found a town and claim land. Free: no fees, no upkeep.

    /town new

  • Homes & teleports

    Save 3 homes. Ask a friend to teleport over.

    /sethome /home /tpa

  • Locked chests

    Chests lock to whoever placed them. Others can ask for access.

Grow

  • Skills

    Mining, farming, fighting: level skills just by playing.

    /skills

  • Chest shops

    Turn a chest into a shop and trade with everyone.

  • Name colors

    Pick a color or gradient for your name.

    /color

Play

  • Tag, you're it

    An always-on game of tag. IT wears a sheep head. Lowest score wins.

    /tag optout to sit out

  • Proximity voice

    Voices get quieter with distance. Needs the voice chat mod.

  • Graves

    No keep-inventory. Your stuff waits in a grave where you died.

  • The End

    Closed for now.
