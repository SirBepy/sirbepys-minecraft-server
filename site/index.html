What's different

Survival like you know it, plus a few friendly extras.

Settle

  • Towns

    Found a town and claim land: 8 chunks per resident, no fees, no upkeep.

    More land
    • The mayor can buy up to 64 extra chunks per town with /t buy bonus, $25 each, paid from the town bank.

    /town new

  • Homes & teleports

    Save 3 homes. Ask a friend to teleport over.

    /sethome /home /tpa

  • Locked chests

    Chests lock to whoever placed them. Others can ask for access.

  • Carry

    Sneak + right-click a passive or friendly mob with an empty hand to carry it on your head, Java and Bedrock alike. Right-click a block to set it down, sneak + left-click to throw it.

    Rules
    • Carrying slows you down and locks your hands: no items, no attacking.
    • Teleporting, logging out or dying sets the mob down where you were.
    • Refused in someone else's town, or on a locked mob without access.
    • A tamed pet can only be carried by its owner.

Grow

  • Skills

    Mining, farming, fighting: level skills just by playing.

    /skills

  • Chest shops

    Turn a chest into a shop and trade with everyone.

  • Name colors

    Pick a color or gradient for your name.

    /color

  • Market

    Six stalls at spawn pick 3 items to buy every sunrise, prices drifting up to 20% either way with a chat announcement. The server buys as little as possible, so farmable items go cheap and hand-gathered ones pay properly.

    Pricing your own shop
    • A Prices book on the Guide stall's lectern lists every stall's base prices.

    /market today's buys /price to suggest one

Play

  • Tag, you're it

    An always-on game of tag. IT wears a sheep head and carries a tracker compass that points at the nearest player (right-click to switch). Anyone idle for 5 minutes is AFK and can't be tagged. Lowest score wins.

    No escaping
    • Within 100 blocks of IT you can't /home, /tpa, /spawn, /back or /warp away. Ender pearls, chorus fruit and portals still work.
    • The new IT can't tag their tagger straight back for 60 seconds, and is told how long is left.

    /tag optout to sit out

  • Dragon Balls

    Three sets of 7: one in the Overworld, one in the Nether, one in the End. Each ball hides inside a structure and floats there, slowly spinning; hit it to pick it up. Gather a full set to summon a dragon and make a wish.

    How the hunt works
    • Overworld balls sit in rooms, on roofs and in streets of villages, towers, ruins and dungeons. Nether and End balls sit in guarded places full of mobs. The End set sleeps until the End opens.
    • Carrying balls slows you down, and they can't go in any container. Die and they drop where you fell, never into your grave. Log off and they drop where you stood.
    • A ball never leaves its own world: go through a portal, /home or /spawn and it stays behind.
    • Each set has its own radar at the Dragon Balls station at spawn: Dragon Radar 1000, Nether Radar 5000, End Radar 20000. It points at the nearest awake ball of its set, even one someone's carrying, but never says how far.
    • Place all 7 of one set within 2 blocks of each other, then hold that set's radar and right-click or hit one to choose your dragon: Shenron grants one great wish, Porunga grants three, each about half as strong. From the Nether set Shenron rises as Nuova Shenron, and deeper sets grant bigger wishes. The radar shatters when the dragon appears.
    • Type your wish in chat and the dragon answers. Ask for too much and it may demand a curse before granting it.
    • After a wish the set flies off within its own world and lands further away each time. Its balls turn to stone, findable but invisible to the radar and unable to summon, for 3 Minecraft days.
    • Java players need the server resource pack, which shows the round balls, the radars and the dragons' looks. Bedrock players see balls as heads, a normal compass and a normal Ender Dragon.

  • Proximity voice

    Voices get quieter with distance. Java only, with the modpack.

  • Graves

    No keep-inventory. Your stuff waits in a grave where you died.

  • Phantoms

    Skip sleep for 2 nights and they come, twice as often as in vanilla: bigger, faster, fireproof, and heard from twice as far. The ones trailing purple swirls poison you; the rest are tougher. A Shenron wish.

  • Adventurer's Guild

    Register for 500 coins and climb ranks E through S with contracts and Promotion Trials. Daily and weekly contracts pay out, alongside server-wide bounties, Grand Quests the whole server tackles together, and a Hall of Fame with titles to earn.

    The Guild Hall
    • ImMijo is the Guildmaster and builds the Guild Hall himself.

    /guild

  • The End

    Closed for now.
